How to get the best results

☀️

Good lighting matters most. Take photos in bright, even light. Avoid shadows across the text.

🎨

Contrast is key. Tesseract works best with dark text on a light background, or light text on a dark background. Low contrast (grey on grey) produces poor results.

🔍

Get close enough. Text should be clearly legible in the photo. If you have to squint to read it, so does the OCR engine.

🖋

Printed text works best. Handwriting is much harder to read accurately, especially cursive. Block handwriting works reasonably well.

🌐

Select the right language. The OCR engine uses language-specific patterns. If your text is in Swedish, select Swedish — even if you plan to translate it afterward.

✂️

Crop out noise. If the image has a lot of background clutter, crop it first so the text fills most of the frame.
